About

G'day, I'm Pat.

Sydney-based partner manager with a thing for planes and the outdoors.

A partner manager in tech by day, an aviation enthusiast and tinkerer by night.

I live in Sydney, and spend my weekends outdoors with family & friends, or learning to fly.

I previously managed Microsoft distributors across Australia and New Zealand, building the systems to drive repeatable growth in their business.

Anchored in my passion for product, learning, and teamwork, I have co-created playbooks, led on technical skilling and experimented so much with AI to rethink how routine things can evolve.

I thrive in a fast-paced environment, even if there's ambiguity or when the goals aren't crystal clear.

Afterall, as a 'plane guy', I like the analogy "Building the plane while flying it"

Previously, I served on the boards of the Corporate Mental Health Alliance of Australia Early-in-Career Committee and Codess.

I love hiking, snowboarding, stargazing, and a nice piccolo.

My motto? Endlessly curious, always learning, and listen before speaking.
